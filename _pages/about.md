I am a tenure-track faculty member at [CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security](https://cispa.de). Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at Aarhus University, hosted by Claudio Orlandi. Before that, I completed my PhD jointly at Reichman University (formerly IDC Herzliya) and Université Paris Cité, advised by Elette Boyle and Geoffroy Couteau.

My research focuses on Cryptography, and more specifically on Secure Multiparty Computation. I am also interested in most problems at the frontier between discrete mathematics and theoretical computer science, including Graph Theory, Combinatorics, and Complexity Theory.

You can find a copy of my PhD thesis [here](https://theses.hal.science/tel-04583917/file/va_Meyer_Pierre.pdf), and the complete list of my publications on [my DBLP page](https://dblp.org/pid/94/1398.html), or browse them below.

I have <b style='color:#FF0000;'>open PhD and Postdoc positions</b> in my group.
I do not answer generic emails.
For research internships or PhD positions in particular, a recommendation (even informal) from someone I know is very useful (but not mandatory).
